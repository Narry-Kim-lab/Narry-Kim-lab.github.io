Youngran Park is a postdoctoral fellow studying the RNA biology of
coronaviruses. Youngran led a 2026 *Nature Communications* study that
identified the genome packaging signal of SARS-CoV-2 in the nsp12-coding
region, and was a co-first author of a 2025 *Nucleic Acids Research* study
showing that the viral protein Nsp2 recruits GIGYF2 near viral replication
sites to support viral protein production. Youngran also contributed to
the SARS-CoV-2 RNA interactome (*Molecular Cell*, 2021).

Before turning to RNA, Youngran studied cancer biology: doctoral research
at Johns Hopkins University on the caretaker role of ARID1A in DNA repair,
which showed that ARID1A-deficient tumor cells are selectively vulnerable
to ionizing radiation combined with PARP inhibitors (*Clinical Cancer
Research*, 2019), followed by work modeling pediatric brain tumors (DIPG)
in human neural stem cells.
