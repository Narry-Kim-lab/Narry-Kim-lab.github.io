Hyejun Kim is a graduate student working where RNA biology meets
computation. Hyejun took part in a 2025 *Nature Microbiology* study showing
that a long non-coding RNA of human cytomegalovirus counteracts nuclear
cGAS, a sensor of foreign DNA, to help the virus evade immunity; the
supplemental code for that study is on Hyejun's GitHub. Earlier, Hyejun led
a study of microRNA profiles in seborrheic dermatitis (*Scientific
Reports*, 2022).

Hyejun also writes small tools for everyday transcriptome analysis, such
as utilities for GENCODE annotations and for building clean reference sets
of mRNAs.
