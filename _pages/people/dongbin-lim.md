Dongbin Lim is a graduate student studying RNA elements that control
how long an mRNA lives and how much protein it makes. Dongbin contributed
to the lab's 2026 *Cell* atlas of vertebrate viral RNA elements, which
identified sequences that stabilize RNA and enhance translation, elements
that may help make mRNA medicines more effective.
