Chemin Lee is a graduate student who studies how short stretches of RNA
decide the fate of the mRNAs that carry them. Viruses are full of such
elements, evolved to keep their RNAs stable and their proteins coming, and
Chemin searches among them for ones that work in vertebrate cells.

Chemin is a co-first author of a 2026 *Cell* study that built a functional
atlas of RNA elements from vertebrate viruses, identifying sequences that
stabilize RNA and enhance translation: a resource for designing mRNAs that
last longer and make more protein.
