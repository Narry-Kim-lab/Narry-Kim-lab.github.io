Seongjae Ken Kim is a graduate student with the rare power of seeing
RNA fold in slow motion, first discovered while staring into a pot of
noodles. Ken now puts it to use on molecules several billion times smaller,
and with somewhat fewer noodles.

Ken's lab coat has a cape attached. Ken insists it improves
aerodynamics.
