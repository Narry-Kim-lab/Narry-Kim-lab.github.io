Junyoung Park is a graduate student studying how microRNAs are made.
In the nucleus, the Microprocessor, DROSHA and DGCR8 together, crops each
primary microRNA, and helper proteins tune how well it does so. Junyoung is
an author of a 2025 *Nature Communications* study showing that the protein
ERH promotes primary microRNA processing well beyond its known role of
assisting microRNAs in clusters.
