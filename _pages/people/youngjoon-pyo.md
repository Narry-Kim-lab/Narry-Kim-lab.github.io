Youngjoon Pyo is a graduate student studying how cells recognize RNA
that does not belong to them. Youngjoon is an author of a 2025 *Science*
study on exogenous RNA surveillance by the proton-sensing protein TRIM25,
which revealed a way for cells to tell foreign RNA, such as that of
viruses, from their own.

## Current project

**Operation Proton Detector.** Building a pocket-sized gadget that
beeps whenever a foreign RNA enters the cell, inspired by TRIM25's keen
nose for acidity. The prototype currently beeps at lemons.
