Seong-In Hyun is a postdoctoral fellow with a background in virology
and human genetics. Seong-In's doctoral research, with Bernard Moss,
examined how vaccinia virus builds its virions, from the topology of viral
membrane proteins (*Traffic*, 2015) to the role of the I2 protein in virion
morphogenesis (*Journal of Virology*, 2017). Seong-In then worked in
clinical genomics, on exome sequencing for the diagnosis of rare genetic
disorders in many thousands of patients.

In the lab, Seong-In has contributed to the discovery of exogenous RNA
surveillance by the proton-sensing TRIM25 (*Science*, 2025) and of RNA
stability enhancers for durable base-modified mRNA therapeutics (*Nature
Biotechnology*, 2025).
