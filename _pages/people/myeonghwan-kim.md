Myeonghwan Kim is a postdoctoral fellow who studies how cells handle
RNA, both their own and that of invaders. Myeonghwan led a 2025 *Science*
study on exogenous RNA surveillance by TRIM25, a protein that senses
protons, revealing a way for cells to recognize foreign RNA, and was a
co-first author of a study showing that the LARP1–PABP complex protects
short poly(A) tails from deadenylation, a critical determinant of mRNA decay (*Nature Structural & Molecular
Biology*, 2023).

Myeonghwan has also contributed to work on the long non-coding RNAs of
human cytomegalovirus (*Scientific Reports*, 2022) and on innate immune
responses to mRNA vaccines (*Nature Communications*, 2024).
