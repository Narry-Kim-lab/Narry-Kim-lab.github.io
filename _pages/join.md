# Join us

## Postdoctoral researchers

**Research on RNA biology and the development of RNA therapeutic technologies**

Our laboratory seeks to uncover the fundamental principles of RNA
regulation and, building on them, to develop new RNA technologies that
control gene expression precisely.

To this end, we carry out integrative research spanning biochemistry,
molecular and cell biology, bioinformatics, and structural biology, aiming
to understand how RNA works and to extend that understanding to
therapeutic applications.

We value scientific rigor and creativity, and we strive for a
collaborative research environment. We are looking for postdoctoral
researchers who share our vision and want to grow and take on challenges
with us.

The researchers we appoint will work on the following topics:

- **Decoding the molecular features of human mRNA** that determine RNA
  stability and translation efficiency through integrative analyses of
  sequencing data and biochemical assays.
- **Developing deep learning models** trained on high-quality, large-scale
  experimental datasets to predict sequence–function relationships in mRNA
  regulation.
- **Identifying and characterizing RNA-regulatory proteins** that regulate
  mRNA stability using proteomic, biochemical, and molecular biological
  approaches.
- **Screening viral RNA elements** that modulate gene expression through
  sequencing-based functional viromic screens.
- **Elucidating the mechanisms of viral RNA elements** and applying these
  insights to understand the logic of human mRNA regulation.
- **Developing novel genetic tools** based on human and viral RNAs for
  next-generation RNA therapeutics.

## Qualifications

- A PhD, or one expected soon, in molecular biology, chemistry,
  bioinformatics, structural biology, or a related field
- The ability to carry out research independently and to work with others

Please send the following to Hyojin Park

- A CV, including a list of publications
- A statement of research interests and a cover letter
